Important Educational Project Notice

CycleCare is designed as an educational web-development project demonstrating full-stack concepts (Flask, MySQL, and REST APIs). Upcoming cycle predictions are mathematical estimates based strictly on user-entered historical averages. CycleCare is not a medical device and should never be used for clinical diagnosis, medical treatment, or contraception planning.
